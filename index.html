My Soundboard — Support

My Soundboard is a free iPhone app: twelve pads, each with a sound. Tap a pad to play it (it fires the moment your finger touches), and press and hold a pad to edit it.

How to

  • Play a sound: tap a pad. Several pads can play at the same time.
  • Stop everything: the Stop key at the bottom.
  • Mute: the Mute key at the bottom. The app plays through the ring/silent switch, so this is the mute to use.
  • Change a pad: press and hold it. Rename it, pick a color and an icon, adjust its volume, choose a built-in sound, pick an audio file from the Files app, or record a new sound with the microphone.
  • Add your own sounds: press and hold a pad → "Choose a file", or share an audio file to My Soundboard from any app.
  • Reset: the ⋯ menu at the top right → "Reset to built-in sounds".

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Privacy

The app collects no data. See the privacy policy.
