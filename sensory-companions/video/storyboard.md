# Demo video storyboard — Sowala · Weighted Sensory Companions

1920×1080 · 30 fps · 59.5 s · H.264 yuv420p + AAC 160 k · **`demo-en.mp4` (English only — no other language versions).**
Scene sources: `scenes/sNN-*.html`, shared `scenes/common.css`. Each scene is one seekable CSS timeline (absolute `animation-delay`s), captured with `frames.js`, concatenated, then muxed with the synthesized music bed. Every scene's out-fade ends 0.15 s before the cut, so each cut lands on a clean cream frame (dip-to-cream transitions); the end card holds to the last frame.

Photo assets: `assets/clean/` — the sloth pair, red-panda pair, small sloth and small red panda are re-cut from `group-cutout.png` with feathered polygon masks that follow the plush outline (no straight crop edges, no fragments of the neighbouring animal); `group-sloth-first.png` is the mirrored group cutout so the sloth leads.

## Music
Synthesized in numpy/scipy (`video/music.py`, run as `python3 music.py 59.5 music.wav`): slow I–vi–IV–V pad in F major (F · Dm · B♭ · C, 6 s per chord), three detuned triangle+sine voices per note with a soft octave shimmer, 2nd-order low-pass and a slow 9 s amplitude "breath"; sparse music-box style pentatonic plucks (decaying sines) every 1.5–3 s; 12 ms stereo offset for width; 1.5 s fade-in, 4 s fade-out; normalised to −18 dBFS RMS (peak −4.3 dBFS, no clipping); exactly 59.5 s.

## Rule check
Every caption and label is on screen ≥ 2.5 s before its out-fade starts (shortest: the s09 footer and the s10 tagline-adjacent items at 2.5 s). Copy uses only the allowed positioning language from BRIEF §3 (no condition, therapy, clinical, medical, proven or sleep claims; "therapist" appears only as *inspired by a therapist*); weights are always "prototype target"; the brand is "Sowala" only; the end card carries the tagline and the origin line; the story beat carries the audience line.
