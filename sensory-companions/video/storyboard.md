# Demo video storyboard — Sowala · Weighted Sensory Companions

1920×1080 · 30 fps · 59.5 s · H.264 yuv420p + AAC 160 k · **`demo-en.mp4`** — English only, one version, brand "Sowala" only.
Scene sources: `scenes/sNN-*.html` + shared `scenes/common.css`. Each scene is one seekable CSS timeline (absolute `animation-delay`s), captured with `frames.js` at 30 fps, concatenated, then muxed with the synthesized music bed. Every scene's out-fade ends 0.15 s before the cut, so each cut lands on a clean cream frame (dip-to-cream); the end card holds to the last frame.

Photo assets: `assets/clean/` — cut by `assets/clean/cutouts.py` from `group-cutout.png` (alpha) + `store-photo.jpg` (colour). Each plush is masked with a hand-traced polygon that follows its **own** outline, so nothing from the neighbouring animal leaks in: the small red panda's right arm is cut at its own edge (the big panda's curly paw, the sloth-arm shadow and the store tag are out), the sloth pair's seam runs down the big sloth's arm and around its three white claws (the dark shadow wedge under the claws and the white fluff patch beside the arm are alpha 0), the red panda pair's seam hugs the big panda's mane and ear (no blue bag / grey sloth band), and the small sloth's right paw with its white felt claws is restored from the store photo. Uniform 2.5 px feather, 3 px alpha erosion, de-fringed edges, no detached fragments.

## Music
Synthesized in numpy/scipy (`video/music.py`, run as `python3 music.py 59.5 music.wav`): slow I–vi–IV–V pad in F major (F · Dm · B♭ · C, 6 s per chord), three detuned triangle+sine voices per note with a soft octave shimmer, 2nd-order low-pass and a slow 9 s amplitude "breath"; sparse music-box style pentatonic plucks every 1.5–3 s; 12 ms stereo offset for width; 1.5 s fade-in, 4 s fade-out; normalised to −18 dBFS RMS (peak −4.3 dBFS, no clipping); exactly 59.5 s.

## Rule check
Timing audit (script over every `--i`/`--o` in the scene files, nested rows, pills, chips and labels included; "fully legible" = from the end of the 0.55 s fade-in to the start of the fade-out; elements that hold to a cut count to the cut): every caption, label, pill, chip and footnote is fully opaque for ≥ 2.5 s. Copy uses only the allowed positioning language from BRIEF §3 (no condition, therapy, clinical, medical, proven or sleep claims; "therapist" appears only as *inspired by a therapist*; "temperature-limited" does not appear); weights are always "prototype target"; the brand is "Sowala" only (no other brand word anywhere); the end card carries the tagline and the origin line; the story beat carries the audience line. All text sits inside the 80 px safe margins.
