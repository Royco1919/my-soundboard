## Music
Synthesized in numpy/scipy (`video/music.py`, run as `python3 music.py 59.0 music.wav`): slow I–vi–IV–V pad in F major (F · Dm · B♭ · C, 6 s per chord), three detuned triangle+sine voices per note with a soft octave shimmer, 2nd-order 900 Hz low-pass and a slow 9 s amplitude "breath"; sparse music-box style pentatonic plucks (decaying sines) every 1.5–3 s; 12 ms stereo offset for width; 1.5 s fade-in, 4 s fade-out; normalised to −18 dBFS RMS (peak −4.6 dBFS, no clipping); exactly 59.0 s.

## Captions rule check
Every caption card is on screen ≥ 2.5 s (shortest: "Kids pick the animal by the feel they like", 2.7 s). Copy uses only the allowed positioning language from BRIEF §3 (no condition, therapy, clinical, medical, proven or sleep claims); weights are always "prototype target".
