01 · Weight A

A hug that stays

Removable weighted pouch in the lower belly: deep, even pressure. Double-sealed, out of reach in normal use — lift it out to wash.

02 · Warm & Cool B

Chill it or warm it

A second pocket, its own insert: chill the reusable gel pack or warm the microwaveable grain insert made for plush. No wires, no batteries.

03 · Touch

Textures to explore

Every friend feels different: curly, waffle, ribbed, long-pile, smooth. Hidden texture patches on belly, paws and ears — it still looks like a friend.

04 · Calm & Fidget

One signature feel

Sloth: extra-long hugging arms. Red Panda: crinkle tail and ribbed pads. Kids pick their friend by the feel they like best.

Inside the belly two hidden pockets · two removable inserts

  • AWeighted pouch — double-sealed poly pellets, out of reach in normal use, lifts out to wash.
  • BWarm & cool insert — gel pack for the fridge, or a microwaveable grain insert made for plush.
  • Never one pouch — weighted and thermal materials each get their own pocket. Outer plush is machine-washable once both are out · spare inserts available.
