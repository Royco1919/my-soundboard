Weighted Sensory Companions

One companion.
Four kinds of comfort.

A weighted sensory companion with a warm & cool comfort insert and a whole body of textures to explore. Designed with children on the autism spectrum in mind.

Calm & Fidget
One signature feel

Sloth: extra-long hugging arms that wrap around and fasten with child-safe hook-and-loop. Red Panda: squeezable crinkle tail and ribbed paw pads to rub.

Warm & Cool
Chill it or warm it

A second pocket with its own insert: chill the reusable gel pack or warm the microwaveable grain insert made for plush. No wires or batteries.

Touch
Textures to explore

Every animal feels different: long-pile fur, textured claws and a raised-dot patch on the Sloth; curly mane, silky ears, ribbed paw pads and a waffle-knit belly on the Red Panda.

Weight
A hug that stays

Removable weighted pouch in the lower belly gives deep, even pressure. Double-sealed, out of reach in normal use — slide it out to wash.
