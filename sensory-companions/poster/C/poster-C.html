01 · Weight

A hug that stays

Removable weighted pouch in the lower belly: deep, even pressure. Double-sealed, out of reach in normal use — slide it out to wash.

02 · Warm & Cool

Chill it or warm it

A second pocket, its own insert — it never shares a pouch with the weight. Chill the reusable gel pack, or warm the microwaveable, temperature-limited insert. No wires, no batteries.

03 · Touch

Textures to explore

Every friend feels different: curly, waffle, ribbed, long-pile, smooth. Hidden texture patches on belly, paws and ears, waiting to be found.

04 · Calm & Fidget

Pick by feel

Sloth: extra-long hugging arms. Red Panda: crinkle tail and ribbed paw pads. Kids choose the animal by the feel they like.

Both inserts lift out · outer plush is machine-washable · spare inserts available
