01 · Weight

A hug that stays

Removable weighted pouch in the lower belly — deep, even pressure. Double-sealed, out of reach in normal use.

02 · Warm & Cool

Chill it or warm it

Second pocket, its own insert: reusable gel pack from the fridge, or a microwaveable grain insert made for plush. No wires, no batteries.

03 · Touch

Textures to explore

Every friend feels different — curly, waffle, ribbed, long-pile, smooth, with hidden texture patches.

04 · Calm & Fidget

Pick by feel

Sloth: hugging arms. Red Panda: crinkle tail and ribbed pads. Kids choose the animal by the feel they like.

Both inserts lift out · outer plush is machine-washable · spare inserts available
