01 · Weight A

A hug that stays

Removable weighted pouch in the lower belly: deep, even pressure. Double-sealed, out of reach in normal use — lift it out to wash.

02 · Warm & Cool B

Chill it or warm it

A second pocket, its own insert: chill the reusable gel pack, or warm the microwaveable, temperature-limited insert. No wires, no batteries.

03 · Touch

Textures to explore

Every friend feels different: curly, waffle, ribbed, long-pile, smooth. Hidden patches on belly, paws and ears.

04 · Calm & Fidget

One signature feel

Sloth: extra-long hugging arms, child-safe hook-and-loop. Red Panda: crinkle tail and ribbed pads. Kids pick by feel.

Comfort, one hug at a time.
