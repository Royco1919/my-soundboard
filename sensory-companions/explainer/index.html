The Story of Sowala

Sowala began with a simple act of love.

For years, a therapist dedicated to helping children on the autism spectrum would call her husband while he was out and ask, Can you pick up another sensory toy for one of my kids? More often than not, she was looking for something soft, comforting, and especially a sloth, because her clients loved holding and cuddling them.

Her husband began noticing how much these simple toys meant to the children. They weren’t just stuffed animals—they could provide comfort, security, and a calming sensory experience.

That inspired an idea: What if he could create a sensory companion designed specifically with these children in mind?

And that’s how Sowala was born—a soft, lovable sensory friend designed with different textures to touch and explore, comforting weight to hold close, and a removable warm‑or‑cool insert for an added soothing experience.

Sowala was inspired by a therapist, created with love, and made to bring comfort—one hug at a time.

The system

One companion, four kinds of comfort.

Every Sowala combines four sensory functions in one plush. All inserts are removable, so the outer animal goes in the washing machine.

What’s inside

Two pockets, two inserts, one washable plush.

An exploded view of the lower belly. The weighted pouch and the thermal insert live in separate pockets behind one hidden flap—so each can be removed, replaced or left out on its own.

  1. 1
    Outer plush

    The animal itself: long‑pile or curly fur, embroidered face, no loose parts. With both inserts removed it goes in the washing machine.

  2. 2
    Hidden belly flap

    One fur‑covered opening on the lower belly, closed with child‑safe hook‑and‑loop under the seam. It is not obvious to a child in normal play and needs an adult’s deliberate pull.

  3. 3
    Pocket B · warm‑or‑cool insert

    The upper pocket takes either the reusable gel pack straight from the fridge or the microwaveable, temperature‑limited insert made for plush products. No electronics anywhere in the toy.

  4. 4
    Pocket A · weighted pouch

    The lower‑belly pocket holds a double‑sealed pouch of poly pellets, so the weight sits low and the animal settles into the lap. Prototype target 3 lb (Large) / 2 lb (Small), to be tuned in testing.

  5. 5
    Divider seam

    A stitched wall between the two pockets. Thermal and weighted materials never share a pouch—easier to make, clean, replace and test.

Why two pockets and not one? Keeping the thermal insert separate from the weighting material makes manufacturing, cleaning, replacement and safety review much simpler—each insert can be swapped or sold as a spare on its own.

How it works

Chill or warm. Slide in. Hug.

Three steps, all done by an adult. The weighted pouch stays in for everyday play; the thermal insert goes in when it’s wanted.

Step 1

Chill or warm the insert

Gel pack in the fridge for cool; the temperature‑limited insert in the microwave for warm. An adult checks it is comfortable on their own skin first.

Step 2

Slide it into the belly pocket

Open the hidden flap, slide the insert into its own pocket above the weighted pouch, and press the flap closed.

Step 3

Hug

The weight settles low in the lap, the insert warms or cools the belly, and the long arms wrap around. Adult supervision with the warm insert.

Safety & positioning

Say who it’s for. Never promise what it will do.

Sowala names its audience and tells its true origin story. It does not make health claims—a children’s product that involves weight and a heated component has to be described carefully and tested properly.

What we say

  • Weighted sensory companion
  • Warm & cool comfort insert
  • Multiple tactile textures
  • Designed for sensory exploration and comfort
  • Calming deep‑pressure feel · cozy, soothing, grounding
  • Designed with children on the autism spectrum in mind
  • Inspired by a therapist who works with children on the autism spectrum
  • Sensory‑friendly · a sensory friend

What we never say

  • That it treats, helps, relieves, reduces or improves autism, ADHD, anxiety, meltdowns, stress, pain, sleep, focus or sensory processing
  • Therapeutic, clinical, medical, doctor‑recommended, therapist‑approved
  • Proven, scientifically, clinically
  • Any sleep claim—it is not a sleep product
  • “Helps with anxiety”, “calms meltdowns”, “regulates”

Health‑outcome claims on a consumer product require competent and reliable scientific evidence under FTC rules. Saying who it is designed for is allowed; saying what it will do to them is not.

Regulatory checklist (U.S.)

  1. ASTM F963 toy safety standardThe mandatory U.S. toy standard under CPSIA. Covers mechanical, seam and stuffing requirements, plus the small‑parts and flammability tests a plush with inserts has to pass.
  2. CPSIA third‑party testingTesting at a CPSC‑accepted laboratory, with a Children’s Product Certificate issued before the product is sold. Includes lead and phthalate limits for the fabrics, pellets and inserts.
  3. Small parts & pellet containmentAges 3+ grading; the pellet pouch is double‑sealed and not reachable in normal use, seams are tested for stuffing and pellet release, and no magnets are used anywhere.
  4. Tracking labelPermanent CPSIA tracking information on the plush and on each insert: manufacturer, production location and date, and batch—so a single insert can be traced or recalled.
  5. Thermal insert reviewThe microwave insert is a temperature‑limited product made for plush; the final design still needs its heating instructions, surface temperatures and misuse cases reviewed. Sources for each item will be added in a later step.

Manufacturing notes

Built to be washed, refilled and repaired.

Materials

  • Outer: long‑pile plush (Sloth) or short plush with curly‑pile mane (Red Panda), embroidered eyes and nose—no hard eyes.
  • Texture patches: minky, corduroy, satin, sherpa, raised‑dot fabric, waffle knit—all sewn in, none glued.
  • Weighting: poly pellets in a double‑sealed inner bag, then a fabric outer pouch.
  • Thermal: reusable gel pack; microwaveable temperature‑limited insert designed for plush.

Closures

  • One hidden belly flap with wide, soft hook‑and‑loop under a fur‑covered seam.
  • No magnets, no zipper pulls, no buttons or beads.
  • Sloth arm fastening: child‑safe hook‑and‑loop at the claws, sized so a child can pull free.

Washability

  • Both inserts out → outer plush is machine‑washable on a gentle cycle, air dry.
  • Weighted pouch: wipe clean. Gel pack: wipe clean. Microwave insert: follow the maker’s care instructions.

Spare inserts

  • Weighted pouch, gel pack and microwave insert each sold separately as spares and replacements.
  • One insert size fits both Large and Small pockets; only the pellet weight differs.

Next steps

From concept to a tested product.

Now

Prototype

Sew the two pockets, the flap and the texture patches into the existing plush. Build 2 lb and 3 lb pouches and the two thermal inserts.

Then

Wear test

Families try both sizes at home. Tune the pouch weight instead of defaulting to heavier, check the flap stays closed, and see which textures get used most.

Before sale

Accredited lab test

ASTM F963 and CPSIA testing at a CPSC‑accepted lab, Children’s Product Certificate, tracking labels, and a review of the warm insert’s instructions.
