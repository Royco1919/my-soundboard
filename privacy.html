My Soundboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

My Soundboard is a free iPhone app made by Roy Cohen. It plays sound clips from a grid of pads.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of its own. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you choose to record a sound onto a pad. Recordings are saved on your device only and never leave it. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Files you import

Audio files you add from the Files app or the share sheet are copied into the app's private folder on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Data storage

Your pads, labels, colors, and sounds are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
